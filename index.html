What software and enterprise platforms does MuregiScore Technologies engineer? +

MuregiScore Technologies is a multi-disciplinary engineering umbrella company in Nairobi, Kenya. We design and deploy high-performance software across six specialized divisions:

  • Business Software (Core CRM): Multi-tenant client management, sales pipelines, role-based access control, and asynchronous job queues (BullMQ + Redis) for SMEs.
  • Education Technology (EduCore & Ratiba Solver): End-to-end institution administration for CBC and 8-4-4 schools, automated student billing, and algorithmic timetable scheduling eliminating room and teacher clashes.
  • Payments & Integrations: Direct Safaricom Daraja M-Pesa rails, sub-second STK Push, real-time C2B Paybill & Till webhooks, and automatic ledger reconciliation.
  • Industry-Specific Systems: Livestock Management SaaS tracking animal passports, pedigree lineages, and daily milk yield variance analytics.
  • Websites & Digital Platforms: High-speed, search-optimized commercial portals engineered for lead conversion and zero latency.
  • Bespoke Engineering: Custom relational schema modeling, constraint optimization solvers, and high-availability SLAs for non-standard enterprise challenges.
How does MuregiScore engineer Safaricom Daraja M-Pesa payment integrations? +

We engineer direct REST API bridges connecting client software directly to Safaricom's Daraja gateway rails:

  • Lipa Na M-Pesa Online (STK Push): Sub-second USSD push prompt delivered straight to the customer's phone for immediate PIN entry.
  • C2B Paybill & Till Webhooks: Immediate validation and confirmation webhook endpoints ensuring instant transaction ingestion.
  • Automated Ledger Reconciliation: Asynchronous workers cross-verify transaction IDs against database invoices without human manual receipt verification.
  • B2C Disbursements: Programmatic payouts for payroll, refund automation, and vendor disbursements.
What makes EduCore and the Ratiba timetable engine compliant with Kenya's CBC curriculum? +

Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) introduces multi-pathway learning areas, continuous assessment tests (formative rubrics), and strict teacher-to-learning-area assignments. EduCore manages these complex records, student portfolios, and fee collections.

Its companion engine, EduCore Ratiba, uses a calibrated genetic constraint-solving engine (FET logic) to schedule multi-stream schools. It guarantees zero room clashes, zero teacher double-bookings, and balanced teacher workloads across every 40-period weekly cycle.

How does the Core CRM architecture model handle multi-tenancy and background queues for SMEs? +

The Core CRM model implements strict tenant schema isolation in relational databases (PostgreSQL/Prisma), preventing any possibility of data cross-contamination between distinct SME accounts.

High-latency operations—such as PDF invoice generation, WhatsApp status alerts, and M-Pesa payment polling—are offloaded to a Redis-backed BullMQ job worker pool. This guarantees sub-50ms API response times for end users.

Can MuregiScore design bespoke software for non-standard industry problems? +
